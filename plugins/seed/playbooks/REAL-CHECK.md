# The real check (5 people, this week)

1. **Find five** people who match the strongest segment — not friends who will be kind. Where they gather: a forum, a shop, a class, a Discord.
2. **Ask about the past**, not the future: "When did this last happen? What did you do? What did it cost you?" (People are bad at predicting what they'd use; good at remembering what they did.)
3. **Show, don't pitch**: the one-screen mockup or a 30-second demo. Watch what they do first.
4. **Ask for a commitment**: a pre-order at the real price, an email sign-up for the beta, or 20 minutes of their time next week. Compliments are not data.
5. **Decide with the kill signal** written before you start (e.g. "fewer than 2 of 5 try it on their own plant"). If it fires, pivot or drop — that is a success.
Record what you learned next to the run (`<run>/realcheck-results.md`) and re-run seed with the corrected segments if needed.
