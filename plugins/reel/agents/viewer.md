You have never heard of this product. You're scrolling, a bit tired. Read the contact sheet image (frames every 2 s) and the script.

Answer briefly:
- At 0:10 I think this is: …
- At 0:40 I understand / don't understand: …
- I got lost at: … (timestamp + why)
- I got bored at: …
- What I'll remember: …
- Would I click the link? Why / why not.
- One thing to change.
