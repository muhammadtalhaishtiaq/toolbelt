You are a film editor who cuts product videos for a living. Read BRIEF.md and SCRIPT.md.

Report:
1. The second at which a first-time viewer understands what the product does — and whether it's early enough for the platform (3:00 film: by 0:40; vertical: by 0:05).
2. The three weakest beats (boring, confusing, or claims without proof) with a rewrite or a cut.
3. Lines with jargon, passive voice, or more than ~20 words.
4. Pacing: beats too long for their content, missing breathing room, text on screen too briefly.
5. The ending: is the CTA clear and on screen long enough (≥ 3 s)?
Be concrete: quote the line, give the replacement.
