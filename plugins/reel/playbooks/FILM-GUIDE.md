# Film guide

The rules that made the Showhow demo film (1:53, 3 drafts) work, generalised.

## Truth
1. **Real footage for every product claim.** Captures of the live app with real model calls. Waits are cut and labelled "⏩ 60 s" — never hidden, never faked.
2. **AI art is labelled** ("Illustration by AI") and never used as evidence of what the product does.
3. **Honest failures are content.** If the product says "no confident match" or blocks unsafe input, show it — it builds trust.
4. **Filming finds bugs.** Expect them (Showhow found three: an offset overlay, a hang on phones, a file type greyed out). Fix the product, then re-capture.

## Craft
5. **Capture at 2×** (3840×2160 for a 1080p film). Blurry 1× footage was the biggest problem in draft 1.
6. **Zoom ≤ 1.25× on footage.** More than that looks soft and seasick.
7. **Magic moment early** — by 0:40 in a 3-minute film, in the first 5 s of a vertical cut.
8. **Voice-led edit.** Time beats to the real VO durations; pauses ≤ 0.3 s after questions (0.45 felt slow).
9. **A storyboard that plays** before capturing anything — the cheapest place to change the film.
10. **Icons and logos as SVG or 4× rasters** — small blurry icons ruin a frame.
11. **Music under, never over** the voice; credits in the description.
12. **Drafts are cheap, finals are not** — render drafts at 1080p, review, fix every note, then render the final.

## Workflow
brief → story → **board (approve)** → capture → voice → sound → compose → QA → **draft rounds (approve)** → publish kit.
