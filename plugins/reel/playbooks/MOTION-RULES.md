# Motion rules

- **One motion idea per scene.** A push-in, a reveal, or a callout — not all three.
- **Easing:** ease-out for things arriving (0.3–0.6 s), ease-in for leaving (0.2–0.4 s); never linear for UI elements.
- **Punch-ins on footage:** ≤ 1.25×, 0.6–1.2 s, aimed at the element being discussed; hold, then release.
- **Callouts point at the real UI** (outline, arrow, spotlight), appear just before the VO names the thing, and leave when the VO moves on.
- **Cursor:** visible, moves on a gentle curve, a soft ripple + tick on click. Hide it when nothing is being clicked.
- **Text:** on screen ≥ 1.5 s + 0.3 s per word; ≤ 7 words per title; never two text animations at once.
- **Transitions:** cuts by default; a match-cut or whip only where it carries meaning (before → after).
- **Safe areas:** 16:9 keep text inside 90% width/height; 9:16 keep the centre 80% clear of platform UI (bottom 20% especially).
- **Captions:** 2 lines max, ≤ 42 characters per line, high contrast plate, burned in for social.
- **End card:** product name, URL, repo or handle — held ≥ 3 s with the music resolving.
- **Reduced chaos:** if a frame needs a legend to understand, simplify the frame.
