# Licences for film audio and art

Use only material whose licence you can link. Save the link and the attribution line in `film/audio/CREDITS.md`.

| Source | Licence | What you must do |
|---|---|---|
| Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) | CC BY 4.0 | Credit exactly: `"<Title>" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License` |
| Kenney (kenney.nl) sound packs | CC0 | Nothing required; credit is kind |
| Free Music Archive | varies per track (CC BY, BY-SA, BY-NC…) | Check each track; avoid NC for commercial use, ND for edits |
| Pixabay Music / Sound effects | Pixabay Content License | Free use incl. commercial; check the track page; no re-selling the file alone |
| YouTube Audio Library | per track ("attribution not required" or with attribution) | Only for YouTube uploads; copy the attribution if shown |
| Your own Kokoro voice | Kokoro model licence (Apache-2.0 model weights) | Say it's an AI voice |
| AI images you generated | the generator's terms | Label as AI in the film and description |

Never use: commercial songs, "no copyright" re-uploads, tracks without a licence page, stock footage of real people without a release.
