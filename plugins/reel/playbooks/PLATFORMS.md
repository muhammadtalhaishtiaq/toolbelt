# Platforms

| Platform | Aspect | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hackathon submission | 16:9 | the event's limit (often 3:00) | YouTube unlisted/public as required; check it plays logged-out |
| YouTube | 16:9 | any | Thumbnail 1280×720; chapters from beat times; "not made for kids" |
| X | 16:9 or 9:16 | ≤ 2:20 for most accounts | Attach the video natively; first post carries the hook and link |
| LinkedIn | 16:9 or 1:1 | ≤ 10 min (keep ≤ 2) | Captions on; first frame must read without sound |
| Shorts / Reels / TikTok | 9:16, 1080×1920 | ≤ 60 s (keep ~35 s) | Centre 80% safe; captions always; hook in 1 s |
| Product page | 16:9, muted autoplay | 20–40 s loop | No VO dependency; text carries it |

Export: H.264 High, yuv420p, 30 fps (60 for fast UI), AAC 48 kHz 192 kbps, −14 LUFS for social, −16 for web.
